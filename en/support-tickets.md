# Support

Support is where account-related topics with the Komuta support team are handled through tickets. Each ticket keeps its own conversation history, and the support team's replies accumulate in that same ticket. It's opened from the **[Support](https://console.komuta.io/account/support)** tab on the account page.

![Support tab — ticket list and the selected ticket's conversation](https://cdn.komuta.io/docs/tr/images/support/tickets.png)

---

## Creating a New Ticket

A new ticket is created with **Create Ticket**, filling in three fields:

- **Subject** — the ticket's subject, 3–256 characters.
- **Priority** — how urgent the request is; chosen from **Low**, **Normal**, **High**, and **Critical**, defaulting to **Normal**.
- **Message** — the first message describing the issue, 3–4000 characters.

The created ticket is assigned a ticket number, and the conversation is tracked under that number.

![New ticket window — Subject, Priority, and Message fields](https://cdn.komuta.io/docs/tr/images/support/create-ticket.png)

---

## Conversation and Replies

A reply is sent from the field below the conversation; it can be 3 to 4000 characters.

A closed ticket can't be replied to.

---

## Ticket Statuses

Status is managed by the support team; the only status change the account side can make is closing the ticket. The ticket list can be filtered by these statuses and by priority.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | The ticket has been created and not yet handled. |
| **In Progress** | The support team is working on the ticket. |
| **Awaiting Customer** | Information is needed from the account to continue; it won't proceed until a reply comes in. |
| **Resolved** | The issue has been marked as resolved. |
| **Closed** | The ticket is closed and no longer accepts new messages. |

---

## Closing a Ticket

A ticket whose issue is resolved is closed with **Close Ticket**. A closed ticket moves to the **Closed** status, stops accepting new messages, and can't be reopened — if help is needed again for the same issue, a new ticket is created.
